**How to read the marks**
- **bold** = lean on that word a little
- `/` = short breath (about half a second)
- `//` = full pause (about one second)
- `↗` = voice goes up, like a question
- `[ ]` = how to say it

## Recording tips
- Quiet room; soft furniture around you helps. Phone about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly to the side so your breath doesn't hit it.
- Record the whole thing in one take. If you mess up a line, pause two seconds and say it again; I'll cut the bad one.
- Leave two seconds of silence at the start and end.
- Drink water before; smile slightly while talking, it really comes through.
- Any format works (WhatsApp voice note, m4a, mp3).
